01

Whistle Podu

Original Clue
"5 stars, 1 whistle"

The whistle cannot be blown from the stands. Speak to the dugout terminal.
Solve Path
1
Open DevTools (F12 → Console) on the Whistle Podu page.
2
Type whistlePodu() — or any alias: whistle(), thala(), csk(). The console returns a JWT-style token.
3
Base64url-decode the second segment of the token (between the two dots). It reveals: {"key":"2010-2023","next":"/vault"}
4
key is the range of CSK's IPL championships. That is the answer.
💡
Reasoning: CSK (Chennai Super Kings) won 5 IPL titles. Their active title-winning years: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023 → the span is 2010–2023.
Answer 20102023

The Third Tung

Premise
A late-night Indonesian neighbourhood watch (Siskamling / Pos Ronda). Sounds are struck on a hollow wooden instrument — a slit drum. Three strikes signals a specific message.
Stage 1 — THE PHOTOGRAPH
1
An image shows a 3 AM neighbourhood scene. "Some warnings are repeated because the first one was ignored."
2
In Indonesian Siskamling tradition, the drum is struck a certain number of times to signal different events. Select (highlight) the invisible white text below the image — it reads: "suara ketukan di pos ronda tengah malam: tiga kali bunyi bilah bambu berongga bergetar..."
3
Count: tiga kali = three times. The code book for pos ronda: 3 strikes = Bahaya (Danger) / Sahur. The answer is the number.
Stage 1 Answer 3
Stage 2 — THE COUNTING
1
Shown: TUNG TUNG TUNG. "Tell what the sound was counting."
2
The answer is not the number of tings — it's what instrument is making the sound. Hover the clue hint; select the hidden text near the pos ronda sign in the background.
3
The hollow wooden slit drum used in Indonesian night watch is called a kentungan (or pentungan).
Stage 2 Answer (Final) kentungan
⚠️
Trap: Typing "sahur", "pocong", "tungtungsahur" or creature-related words triggers a red herring warning. The creature is a decoy.
Final Answer kentungan

Premise
A Formula 1 puzzle. Starts with a driver, escalates through a famous race and its track's dual nicknames, then requires breaking a two-layer cipher.
StageClue / LogicAnswer
Stage 1 — REDLINE ECHO An image of a Formula 1 driver. "Identify him. Follow his numbers." — It's Lewis Hamilton. His career grand prix wins. lewishamilton or 105
Stage 2 — 105 Rain-shot image. "Something's hidden here. Key: mercedes." — A steganography or trivia question about Hamilton's win tally at Mercedes. mercedes or win count variant
Stage 3 — /SENNA Four clues: a Brazilian flag inside a German one, a Portuguese phrase, an IMDb link (tt1587310 = Rush, 2013), and a Notting Hill bet. Three are real, one is a decoy. The film Rush depicts the 1976 Niki Lauda vs James Hunt rivalry. rush
Stage 4 — /NURBURGRING The track has two real nicknames — choose your path. Branch A: greenhell · Branch B: widowmaker
Branch A — THE GREEN HELL "Bergwerk is German for 'mine'." A famous historic Australian gold mine lease nicknamed after it. Two words combined. goldenmile
Branch B — THE WIDOWMAKER Ciphertext: iwkzzzutlziwxlaimdkvciyfzfd. Two layers: Layer 1 = Vigenère with the mine key (goldenmile). Layer 2 = Affine (a=5, b=8, inverse: x = 21·(y−8) mod 26). Read the resulting scene. Decoded scene name
🔧
Affine decode formula: For each letter y (0-indexed), compute x = 21 × (y − 8) mod 26. First undo the Vigenère with key goldenmile.
Final Answer (Branch completion) bergwerk

The Hollow Chime

Premise
Three ASMR audio files. Each has a matching ciphertext file. The audio tells you which cipher and key to use. Decrypt each ciphertext. All three decoded answers point to one person.
Transmission 01 — The Radio Repair Shop
Assets
Audio: radio_repair_shop_asmr.mp3  ·  Ciphertext file: tape_scrap_0x1A.dat
Ciphertext: ZDURIWKHZRUOGV
Cipher: Caesar (shift +3)
Shift back by 3 → WAROFTHERWORLDS
📻
Logic: The audio references the most panic-inducing broadcast in history — Orson Welles' 1938 radio play War of the Worlds. The repair shop makes "three taps, three clockwise turns" — shift of 3. Caesar cipher, shift −3.
DecodedWAROFTHERWORLDS → War of the Worlds
Bridge — Found Note (Interlude)
Ciphertext: OLLPULIGSVSVOKVIH
Cipher: Atbash (A↔Z, B↔Y…)
Decoded → LOOKFORTHEHELPERS
🏘️
Logic: "Look for the helpers" is a famous quote by Fred Rogers (Mr. Rogers). The 1938 broadcast caused panic; Mr. Rogers' voice only ever calmed people. Hint: check /mrrogers.
Bridge Answermrrogers
Transmission 02 — The Painting Roleplay
Assets
Audio: Bob Ross Painting Roleplay.mp3  ·  Ciphertext file: easel_scrap_0x4F.log
Ciphertext: IRJHFMZNLSFRWUPEVHLZ
Cipher: Vigenère, key = BRUSH
Decoded → BOBROSSPAINTING
🖌️
Logic: The 5-letter tool mentioned repeatedly throughout the painting roleplay is a brush. That is the Vigenère key. Apply it to decrypt.
DecodedBOBROSSPAINTING → Bob Ross
Transmission 03 — The Fireplace Story
Assets
Audio: Fireplace Bedtime Story.mp3  ·  Ciphertext file: ash_scrap_0x9C.txt
Ciphertext: RIFFYISSWXCVZU
Cipher: Affine (a=5, b=8, a⁻¹=21)
Formula: x = 21 × (y − 8) mod 26
Decoded → BOBROSSSCENERY
🔥
Logic: The audio whispers constants a=5, b=8 in the embers. Affine cipher inverse formula: x = 21 × (y − 8) mod 26. The result again points to Bob Ross.
Final Stage — THE MAN IN THE SCENERY
Convergence
"Three voices, three tools of comfort — a radio, a paintbrush, a fireplace. All quietly pointing at one man who spent thirty years turning mistakes into scenery."
