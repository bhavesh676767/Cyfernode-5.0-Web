WHAT IF… YOUR CORE PRODUCT WAS COMPLETELY FREE?

What if the product that makes your business valuable could never be charged for?

Your challenge is to build a profitable, sustainable, and scalable business around a core product that is completely free to its normal customers.

Participants must first create a product or service with a strong USP (Unique Selling Proposition) - something valuable enough that customers genuinely want to use it. The catch is that the core offering must remain free of cost to the primary customer.

So where does the money come from?

This is where your entrepreneurial thinking begins.

Design a business model where profitability comes through alternative revenue channels such as businesses paying for access to customers, premium physical ecosystems, marketplace commissions, advertising or sponsorships, partnerships, B2B services, licensing, complementary products, or an entirely original monetization strategy.

However, making something free is only half the challenge.

You must also answer:

Why will customers keep coming back?

Your business should have a strong retention strategy that keeps users engaged and creates long-term value without forcing them to pay for the core product.

Your pitch must demonstrate how your business can acquire customers, deliver the core product for free, generate revenue through other channels, retain its users, remain profitable, and scale over time.

The judges should be left thinking:

“If the best part is free… how is this company making money?”

Deliverables

  • Pitch Presentation: A presentation covering the business concept, core product and USP, target customers, value proposition, revenue streams, business model, customer acquisition strategy, retention strategy, competition, scalability, and future vision.
  • Business Model: Clearly explain how the business generates revenue while keeping its core product completely free for normal customers.
  • Live Pitch: Participants must present their business idea within the specified time limit and answer questions from the judges.
  • Supporting Material: Participants may include prototypes, mockups, branding, customer journeys, financial projections, advertisements, or demonstrations to strengthen their pitch.
  • All submitted files must follow the specified submission formats, naming conventions, and deadlines.

Judging Criteria

Criteria Weightage
Innovation & Originality 25%
Business Model & Monetization 25%
Customer Retention & Value 20%
Feasibility & Scalability 15%
